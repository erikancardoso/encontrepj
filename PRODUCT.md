# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel with a React web client, PostgreSQL, Redis, object storage compatible with S3, and a future React Native/Expo mobile client consuming the same API.

## Users

- Independent professional service providers operating through a Brazilian legal entity.
- Contracting companies and their verified representatives.
- Independent recruiters and recruiting companies supporting specialist selection.
- Platform administrators responsible for verification, moderation, disputes, subscriptions, and audit.

## Product Purpose

EncontrePJ connects companies with specialist service providers for fixed-term engagements. It centralizes matching, availability, applications, individual contracts, private tasks, deliverables, fiscal documents, payment status, and bilateral reputation.

## Positioning

Unlike a general job board, EncontrePJ matches verified specialist profiles to service opportunities using profession, skills, evidence, availability, location, and commercial constraints, then preserves the full contracting and delivery trail in one workspace.

## Operating Context

- Companies publish services with a defined start, end, scope, availability window, skills, acceptance criteria, and payment cadence.
- A publication may hire multiple providers, but each provider receives an individual contract and private task workspace.
- Providers can work for multiple companies and only see their own contracts, tasks, files, messages, invoices, and payments.
- Companies see a consolidated project view across their privately separated provider engagements.
- Optional recruiters may review candidates and deliver a shortlist under a separate paid service contract.
- Payment by task is the default; fortnightly and monthly cycles are supported when agreed by both parties.
- An approved delivery requires the applicable NFS-e before payment release.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Three initial self-service account types: professional, contracting company, and recruiter. Administrative access is never self-selected.
- A user may acquire more than one account type over time.
- Company representatives require separate identity, authority, and signing-power verification.
- Professional matching must distinguish the company's industry from the professional category of the requested service.
- Mandatory platform clauses govern approval deadlines, automatic approval, fiscal-document flow, payment release, and dispute mediation. Parties negotiate the remaining contract content.
- Subscriptions are the platform's principal revenue model; there is no planned platform percentage commission on service value.
- Payment custody and release must be delegated to a suitable regulated payment partner.
- GOV.BR signing may be accepted through a manual download/sign/upload flow; private direct API access is not assumed.
- Service descriptions may be provided by text or audio, transcribed, structured into a brief, and converted into draft tasks only after company review.
- Lateral visibility between providers is forbidden, including through APIs, files, messages, exports, notifications, and predictable URLs.

## Brand Commitments

- Working brand: EncontrePJ.
- Brazilian Portuguese is the primary product language.
- The product should communicate specialist quality, reciprocal security, clarity, and professional independence.

## Evidence on Hand

There are no approved logos, colors, customer testimonials, production metrics, legal templates, or final pricing assets yet. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Eligibility before popularity: show opportunities only to meaningfully compatible professionals.
- Private by default: every provider engagement is isolated even inside a shared company project.
- Agreement before obligation: material scope, schedule, price, or contract changes require explicit acceptance.
- Evidence over promises: verification, version history, acceptance records, fiscal documents, and payment events create trust.
- Safety is not a premium feature: existing contracts, records, disputes, and personal data controls remain accessible regardless of plan.
